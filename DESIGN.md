# Obsidian Rail (Precision Dark Theme)

## Signature Moves
- **Near-black `#0c0d0f` page → `#1a1c1e` card**: Single 4% lightness tonal step.
- **Hairline Chrome**: Every divider, card edge, and row separator is 1px at `rgba(245,246,247,0.08)`.
- **Geist Mono with Tabular-Nums**: Used for all numeric values, IDs, timestamps, and currency amounts.
- **Electric Mint `#5fe3b1`**: Exclusively reserved for the primary CTA, active status pills, and focus ring.
- **Inter 600**: Used for calm, authoritative display headings and structured body copy.
